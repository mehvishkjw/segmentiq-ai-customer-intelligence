Why behavioral segmentation?

The business case for moving beyond broad demographics
1. More relevant messages

Match offers and content to observed customer behavior rather than assuming all customers in an age group respond similarly.

2. Better budget allocation

Prioritise high-value customers, retention opportunities, and segments with promising conversion signals.

3. Measurable learning

Use controlled experiments to determine whether segment-specific messages improve conversion, repeat purchase, and contribution margin.

Recommended measurement framework

Evaluate impact rather than campaign activity alone
Conversion rate

Percentage of targeted customers who complete the desired action.

Repeat purchase rate

Share of customers who return and buy again during the measurement window.

Incremental revenue / margin

Compare campaign outcomes with a suitable holdout group to estimate incremental impact.
